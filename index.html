9th Africa Water Week.
13-17th October 2024 in Cairo, Egypt.

The 9th edition of the Africa Water Week (AWW9) will be hosted by the Arab Republic of Egypt and jointly held with the 7th Cairo Water Week on 13 to 17 October 2024 in Cairo, Egypt. it will feature the 14 ordinary session of the Executive Committee of AMCOW on 16 October 2024. The Council will, among others, consider the outcomes from AWW9 and guide on the necessary interventions to ensure their implementation.
